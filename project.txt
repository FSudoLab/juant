Name of System: JuanT
Scope:
JuanT is a role-based employee and HR operations platform for small and medium-sized organizations. It centralizes employee self-service, HR administration, manager approvals, schedules, leave, documents, support, and access control in one workspace.

The current project is a functional product prototype with demo accounts and interactive role-based screens. The production scope will be delivered in phases. The next production phase would add secure authentication, database persistence, audit logs, notifications, integrations, compliance controls, and subscription billing.

Current prototype status:
- Working now: role-based demo sign-in, employee/HR/manager/system-admin dashboards, navigation, manager schedule controls, employee information editing, HR module access toggles, performance overview screens, and interactive sample settings.
- Planned for production: real authentication, organization and employee database, persistent settings, peer performance submissions, payroll integrations, document storage, notification delivery, audit logs, reporting exports, billing, and complete security/compliance review.

Features:
- Role-based workspaces for employees, HR, managers, and JuanT system administrators.
- Employee self-service for attendance, schedules, leave, requests, payroll and payslips, documents, profile, performance, notifications, and support.
- HR workspace for employee management, organization structure, employee 201 files, documents and forms, recruitment, onboarding, attendance, schedules, leave, overtime, payroll, loans, government contributions, performance, engagement, offboarding, reports, and Ask Juan AI.
- Manager administration for individual employee schedules, weekday or weekend work, work hours, leave allowances, leave approvals, documents, employee information, reminders, and HR support.
- Per-employee schedule customization, including workweek, start time, and end time.
- Employee information editing with manager-controlled edit access.
- Performance rating workflow managed by the company manager, with reviews scheduled every month or every three months.
- Peer feedback from other employees within the same organization, combined with the manager's rating and review comments.
- Configurable performance criteria, rating scales, review history, and employee acknowledgement of results.
- Underperformance alerts and improvement-plan tracking when an employee falls below the company's chosen rating threshold.
- HR escalation workflow for documented performance concerns. The manager can recommend further action, while HR reviews the case and manages any formal termination and offboarding process.
- HR-ready offboarding handoff for employees who require separation, including clearance, asset return, exit interview, and final pay preparation.
- HR sidebar access management, allowing managers to show or hide specific HR modules for HR users.
- Leave approval and decline workflows, holiday visibility settings, attendance and schedule management, and team reminders.
- Documents and forms management for company policies and employee resources.
- Dashboard summaries, pending approvals, team coverage, attendance insights, and operational metrics.
- Responsive web interface designed for desktop and mobile use.

Plan feature clarification:
- Starter includes a basic performance tab for employee goals, feedback, and review history. It does not include formal peer-rating campaigns.
- Growth includes manager-led monthly or quarterly performance reviews, peer ratings within the same organization, improvement plans, and HR escalation.
- Business includes advanced performance analytics, configurable criteria, calibration, audit history, and exports.

Payroll and compliance scope:
- Starter and Growth provide payroll and payslip record-keeping, document storage, and employee visibility. They do not promise automatic statutory payroll computation.
- Business may include payroll computation through validated integrations or a separately scoped payroll add-on.
- SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, and BIR information should be treated as records and reporting support until each calculation and filing integration is validated for the relevant Philippine rules.
- Full payroll computation, tax filing, remittance, and legal compliance require a dedicated implementation review and should not be assumed from the prototype.

Security and privacy:
- JuanT should follow the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) for employee records, payslips, 201 files, and performance information.
- Production requirements include consent and privacy notices, role-based least-privilege access, encryption in transit and at rest, secure authentication, audit logs, retention and deletion policies, backups, breach response procedures, and access reviews.
- Data retention periods and consent requirements should be configurable by organization and aligned with applicable employment, tax, and privacy obligations.

Technology direction:
- Frontend: Next.js, React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS.
- Current data: demo and in-memory UI state for prototyping.
- Planned production data layer: secure relational database, authenticated API, object storage for documents, background jobs for notifications, and payment provider integration.

Target clients:
- Small and medium-sized businesses that need an affordable HR operations system.
- Companies moving from spreadsheets, email, and manual HR processes to a centralized platform.
- Organizations with managers who need direct control over schedules, leave, employee information, and team support.
- Philippine-based companies and other regional organizations requiring attendance, leave, payroll, and employee records management.
- US-based or multi-country companies that need configurable holiday calendars and flexible workweek settings.
- HR teams, people operations teams, department managers, and company administrators.

Subscription/Different Plans included:
Proposed plans:

1. Starter
- For small teams beginning to digitize HR operations.
- Employee profiles, attendance, schedules, leave requests, documents, notifications, basic employee self-service, and basic goals/feedback performance history.
- Up to 25 employees.
- Standard email support.
- Privacy and security baseline included; payroll is record-keeping only.

2. Growth
- For growing companies that need manager and HR workflows.
- Everything in Starter, plus manager dashboards, per-employee schedules, leave approvals, employee information editing, HR access controls, reports, reminders, and document management.
- Includes formal monthly or quarterly performance reviews, same-organization peer ratings, improvement plans, and HR escalation.
- Up to 100 employees.
- Priority support.
- Privacy and security baseline included; payroll and statutory information remain record-keeping only unless an approved integration is added.

3. Business
- For organizations with more advanced HR operations.
- Everything in Growth, plus advanced reports, payroll and reimbursement workflows, organization structure, recruitment and onboarding, performance tracking, audit logs, configurable permissions, and priority onboarding.
- Up to 500 employees.
- Dedicated customer success support.
- Payroll computation and statutory integrations are separately validated and scoped; they are not automatically included by the plan name alone.

4. Enterprise
- For larger or multi-location organizations.
- Custom employee limits, custom permissions, integrations, single sign-on, multi-country configuration, data retention controls, service-level agreements, and dedicated implementation support.
- Custom pricing and contract.

Price per plan:
Proposed starting prices, subject to validation through customer interviews and operating-cost analysis:

- Starter: PHP 2,500 per month for up to 25 employees.
- Growth: PHP 7,500 per month for up to 100 employees.
- Business: PHP 20,000 per month for up to 500 employees.
- Enterprise: Custom quote based on employee count, integrations, support level, and implementation requirements.

Pricing notes:
- Annual billing may include a 15% discount.
- Additional employees can be charged using a per-employee monthly rate that remains high enough to make the next plan upgrade economically attractive. Proposed overage rates: PHP 110 per employee for Starter, PHP 85 per employee for Growth, and PHP 50 per employee for Business.
- A free trial or guided demo can be offered before subscription.
- Final pricing should be confirmed after validating target customer demand, hosting costs, support costs, and required integrations.

Roadmap:
1. Production foundation: authentication, organization setup, database persistence, permissions, audit logs, privacy notices, and backups.
2. Core HR operations: employee records, attendance, schedules, leave, documents, notifications, and manager workflows.
3. Performance and lifecycle: peer ratings, monthly/quarterly reviews, improvement plans, HR escalation, offboarding, clearance, and final pay preparation.
4. Integrations and scale: payroll integrations, statutory reporting support, SSO, billing, multi-country settings, analytics, and mobile improvements.

Success metrics:
- Employee onboarding completion rate.
- Monthly active employees and manager adoption.
- Reduction in manual HR requests, spreadsheets, and email approvals.
- Leave and schedule approval turnaround time.
- Performance review completion rate.
- Time required to complete an offboarding checklist.
- Customer retention, plan upgrades, support response time, and data/security incidents.
