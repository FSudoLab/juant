JUANT PRODUCT DOCUMENTATION
===========================

Purpose
-------
JuanT is a role-based employee and HR operations platform for small and medium-sized organizations. It brings attendance, schedules, leave, employee records, payroll records, documents, approvals, performance, and support into one workspace.

This document describes the intended behavior of every role and every current sidebar tab. The current Next.js app is a functional prototype with demo data and in-memory or browser-local state. Production implementation requires authentication, database persistence, file storage, audit logs, notifications, and permission enforcement on the server.

Global rules
------------
- Every record belongs to an organization and must be isolated from other organizations.
- Every important action records the actor, timestamp, previous value, new value, and reason when applicable.
- Employee data, 201 files, payslips, performance records, and requests require least-privilege access.
- Approval actions must show the request details, supporting files, approver, decision, date, and optional comment.
- Status values should be explicit and filterable. Destructive actions should archive or deactivate records instead of deleting history.
- Notifications should be generated for submissions, approvals, declines, reminders, expiring files, and approaching deadlines.


EMPLOYEE
========

Workspace
---------

Dashboard
  Purpose: Give the employee a quick view of today's workday and open actions.
  Show: time-in status, today's schedule, leave balance, open requests, upcoming leave, notifications, and quick links.
  Actions: time in, time out, view schedule, file a request, open leave, and view notifications.

Time In
  Purpose: Record attendance and correct missing or inaccurate time records.
  Show: time in/out controls, current location or work setup when enabled, daily attendance history, monthly records, and manual request history.
  Actions: time in, time out, view attendance history, submit a manual time-in/time-out request, attach an explanation or evidence, and track approval.
  Rule: A manual attendance request is never final until reviewed and approved by the configured manager or HR approver.

My Schedule
  Purpose: Show the employee's assigned work schedule.
  Show: workdays, start/end time, breaks, location, holidays, weekend work, and schedule changes.
  Actions: view the current schedule and request a schedule change when the organization allows it.

Leave Management
  Purpose: Let employees use and monitor leave credits.
  Show: leave type, total credits, used credits, remaining balance, blackout dates, and leave history.
  Actions: file leave, upload supporting documents, withdraw a pending request, and view approval or decline comments.
  Statuses: Pending, Approved, Declined, Cancelled.

My Requests
  Purpose: Keep every employee-submitted request in one queue.
  Request types: leave, overtime, attendance correction, schedule change, certificate of employment, reimbursement, and other organization-defined requests.
  Show: request ID, type, summary, filed date, approver, status, decision date, and comments.

My Employment
------------

Payroll & Payslips
  Purpose: Give employees access to released payroll records.
  Show: pay period, gross pay, deductions, contributions, tax withholding, net pay, pay date, and payslip download.
  Rule: Draft or unreleased payroll is not visible to employees. Payroll computation and statutory filing must be validated separately for each country.

My Documents
  Purpose: Provide employee-visible policies, forms, certificates, and assigned documents.
  Show: document name, category, version, updated date, status, and download or acknowledgement state.
  Actions: view, download, acknowledge, and submit requested documents.

My Profile
  Purpose: Maintain employee contact and personal information.
  Show: name, contact details, address, emergency contact, employment details, and profile photo where enabled.
  Actions: edit permitted fields and submit restricted changes for manager or HR review.
  Rule: Changes to employment-sensitive fields require approval and an audit entry.

My Performance
  Purpose: Let employees understand goals, feedback, and review history.
  Show: goals, KPI criteria, review cycle, manager rating, peer feedback where enabled, comments, improvement plan, and acknowledgement state.
  Actions: update goals, submit self-review, provide allowed peer feedback, acknowledge results, and view improvement-plan actions.

Support
-------

Notifications
  Purpose: Show time-sensitive events and reminders.
  Show: unread count, event type, source, timestamp, and deep link to the related record.
  Actions: mark read, open the related item, and manage notification preferences.

Ask Juan
  Purpose: Answer employee questions about policies and workspace actions.
  Behavior: Use approved organization documents as sources, protect personal data, and route uncertain or sensitive questions to HR.
  Actions: ask a question, open a cited document, and create an HR support request.

Help & HR Support
  Purpose: Provide support channels and a history of employee help requests.
  Show: HR contact, FAQs, policy links, request status, and response history.
  Actions: create a support request, attach files, reply, and close the request.

Settings
  Purpose: Manage employee preferences.
  Show: notification settings, language, timezone, security, and active sessions.
  Actions: update preferences, change password, sign out other sessions, and request account help.


HR
==

Workspace
---------

Dashboard
  Show: team members, pending requests, attendance percentage, checked-in count, late count, absent count, on-leave count, notifications, and quick action buttons.
  Quick actions: open approval center, review attendance, add employee, publish a document, and view reports.

Time In
  Purpose: Give HR a time-clock and attendance exception workspace.
  Show: time in/out records, attendance history, location or work setup, missing punches, late and absent employees, and attendance trends.
  Actions: review records, submit a manual attendance request for an employee, attach evidence, and view manual-request history.
  Rule: HR may correct a record only through an auditable approved workflow unless the organization explicitly grants direct correction access.

Approval Center
  Purpose: Process requests that require HR review.
  Queues: manual attendance, leave, overtime, holiday or rest-day work, reimbursements, profile changes, and other configured requests.
  Show: pending count, approved/declined counts, turnaround time, requester, request type, dates, evidence, and prior decisions.
  Actions: approve, decline with reason, request more information, and open the employee record.
  Dashboard counters: checked in today, late, absent, and on leave.

People Management
-----------------

Employee Management
  Purpose: Maintain the employee roster.
  Show: employee name, employee number, department, position, employment type, role, schedule, manager, and status.
  Actions: add employee, edit permitted fields, regularize probationary staff, deactivate, reactivate, assign manager, and open the employee record.
  Statuses: Probationary, Regular, Contractual, Active, Inactive.

Organization Structure
  Purpose: Maintain the company reporting structure.
  Department fields: department name, department head, reports to, headcount, open roles, and status.
  Show: organization tree, headcount by department, team leads, reporting lines, and open roles.
  Actions: add department, edit reporting lines, archive, restore, and review span of control.

Employee 201 Files
  Purpose: Track required employee records and compliance documents.
  Show: employee, required file, missing items, submitted count, last updated date, expiration date, and verification status.
  Actions: upload file, request a missing file, verify, reject with reason, replace an expired file, and view file history.
  Statuses: Complete, Incomplete, For review, Expired.

Documents & Forms
  Purpose: Publish employee-facing policies, forms, and templates.
  Fields: document name, category, version, updated date, owner, audience, file, and status.
  Actions: create draft, upload a revision, publish, archive, restore, require acknowledgement, and view download history.
  Statuses: Draft, Published, Archived.

Talent Acquisition
------------------

Recruitment
  Purpose: Manage candidates from application through hiring decision.
  Candidate fields: name, role, source, applied on, contact details, resume, notes, owner, and status.
  Actions: add candidate, move through screening, interview, offer, hired, or rejected, schedule interviews, record feedback, and convert a hired candidate to onboarding.
  Statuses: Applied, Screening, Interview, Offer, Hired, Rejected.

Onboarding
  Purpose: Move new hires through a repeatable first-day and first-month checklist.
  Fields: name, role, start date, department, manager, buddy, checklist, and status.
  Actions: add new hire, assign tasks, upload requirements, start onboarding, complete tasks, and mark the plan complete.
  Statuses: Not started, In progress, Completed.

Time & Attendance
-----------------

Attendance Management
  Purpose: Monitor live attendance and resolve exceptions.
  Show: checked in today, late, absent, on leave, attendance percentage, employee time records, and exceptions.
  Actions: filter by date or department, review evidence, excuse an exception through policy, and send a correction request.

Schedules & Shifts
  Purpose: Define reusable schedules and assign them to employees.
  Fields: shift name, days, start/end time, breaks, location setup, overnight flag, and assigned employees.
  Actions: create, publish, disable, assign, reassign, and manually customize an employee's Monday-to-Friday or weekend schedule.
  Statuses: Draft, Active, Disabled.

Leave Management
  Purpose: Administer leave types, balances, applications, and availability.
  Actions: configure leave policies, review applications, approve or decline, adjust balances with a reason, and view team coverage.
  Show: pending applications, employees on leave today, approved leave, credits, and approval history.

Overtime & Holiday Work
  Purpose: Review additional work before it reaches payroll.
  Show: employee, date, work type, hours, reason, approver, and estimated cost.
  Actions: approve or decline regular overtime, rest-day work, and holiday work; record the reason; and export approved hours to payroll.

Compensation
------------

Payroll
  Purpose: Prepare, process, review, and release payroll runs.
  Show: cutoff, employee count, gross, deductions, contributions, net, pay date, exceptions, and release status.
  Actions: create a run, validate attendance and approved requests, process, resolve exceptions, release, generate payslips, and lock the run.
  Statuses: Draft, Processing, Released, Voided.

Loans & Reimbursements
  Purpose: Manage company loans, government loans, and expense claims.
  Show: employee, type, amount, terms, filed date, approval, balance, and payment state.
  Actions: review evidence, approve, decline, schedule deduction or payment, mark paid, and view outstanding balance.

Government Contributions & Tax
  Purpose: Track contribution and tax records, deadlines, filings, and remittances.
  Records: SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, BIR withholding, applicable period, employee share, employer share, due date, filing date, and remittance reference.
  Actions: prepare report, mark filed, mark remitted, attach proof, and flag overdue items.
  Rule: Treat these as records and reporting support until calculations and filing integrations are separately validated for the applicable Philippine rules.

Employee Development
-------------------

Performance & KPIs
  Purpose: Run review cycles and monitor performance outcomes.
  Show: cycle, employee, reviewer, goals, KPI score, rating, comments, status, and overdue reviews.
  Actions: configure criteria and rating scale, start review, collect manager or peer feedback, complete review, publish results, acknowledge, and create an improvement plan.
  Statuses: Not started, In review, Completed, Acknowledged.

Learning & Development
  Purpose: Manage training programs and completion.
  Fields: program name, category, owner, schedule, enrolled employees, materials, attendance, and completion.
  Actions: create, publish, enroll, start, complete, and export training history.
  Statuses: Scheduled, Ongoing, Completed, Archived.

Engagement & Feedback
  Purpose: Collect pulse surveys, suggestions, recognition, and engagement signals.
  Show: eNPS, response rate, engagement trend, suggestions, recognitions, and anonymity settings.
  Actions: create or close a survey, acknowledge suggestions, respond through a controlled channel, and export aggregated results.
  Rule: Preserve anonymity where promised and never expose individual survey responses without permission.

Employee Lifecycle
------------------

Offboarding & Clearance
  Purpose: Coordinate separation, clearance, asset return, and exit tasks.
  Fields: employee, last day, reason, manager, checklist, assets, exit interview, sign-offs, and status.
  Actions: start offboarding, notify stakeholders, collect department sign-offs, record asset return, complete exit interview, and mark cleared.
  Statuses: In progress, Cleared, Cancelled.
  Rule: Final pay cannot be released until required clearance rules are satisfied or an authorized exception is documented.

Final Pay
  Purpose: Prepare final compensation after separation.
  Show: last day, unpaid salary, leave conversion, approved overtime, deductions, loans, statutory items, clearance status, and net final pay.
  Actions: calculate or import validated amounts, review, approve, release, generate final payslip, and retain the audit record.

Insights & System
-----------------

Reports & Analytics
  Purpose: Turn HR records into operational and compliance insights.
  Reports: headcount, turnover, attendance, leave, overtime, payroll records, recruitment funnel, onboarding completion, performance, training, engagement, and offboarding.
  Actions: filter by date, department, and status; save views; export permitted reports; and schedule report delivery.

Ask Juan AI
  Purpose: Help HR find policies, summarize records, and understand workflow steps.
  Rule: AI must respect role permissions, cite approved sources, avoid making final employment or payroll decisions, and route sensitive questions to authorized HR users.

HR Settings
  Purpose: Configure HR workspace behavior.
  Settings: leave types, approval routing, holidays, workweeks, shifts, document categories, required 201 files, payroll cutoffs, performance cycles, notification rules, and HR module visibility.
  Actions: update configuration, preview impact, save, restore previous settings, and review audit history.

Help & HR Support

SYSTEM ADMIN
===========

Workspace
---------

Dashboard
  Show: active users, role distribution, HR access state, open audit items, recent security events, and system health.
  Actions: manage users, review HR access, open the audit log, and open system settings.

User Access
  Purpose: Manage who can access the organization workspace.
  Show: user name, email, employee link, role, status, last sign-in, and invitation state.
  Actions: invite user, assign role, deactivate, reactivate, reset access, and revoke sessions.
  Rule: Role changes require confirmation and an audit event. Deactivation must preserve historical records.

HR Access
  Purpose: Control which HR modules are visible and usable by HR users.
  Show: HR user, module name, access state, last changed by, and last changed date.
  Actions: enable or disable approval center, employee records, attendance, payroll, performance, lifecycle, reports, Ask Juan, and other HR modules.
  Rule: Access changes must be permission-checked, audited, and applied server-side; hiding a sidebar item is not a security boundary.

System
-------

Audit Log
  Purpose: Provide a tamper-evident history of security and administrative actions.
  Show: actor, action, resource, organization, timestamp, IP or session context where legally permitted, and before/after summary.
  Actions: filter, inspect details, export according to policy, and flag an event for investigation.

System Settings
  Purpose: Configure organization-wide platform controls.
  Settings: organization profile, timezone, locale, country, data retention, security policy, authentication, notification providers, integrations, backups, and billing plan.
  Actions: update settings, test an integration, review configuration history, and manage data export or deletion requests.


Permission model
----------------
Employee users can access their own records and employee-facing documents. Managers can access direct-report information and assigned approvals. HR can access organization-wide people operations according to HR module permissions. System administrators manage access and platform configuration but should not automatically receive unrestricted access to private HR content.

Production foundation checklist
-------------------------------
- Secure authentication, password recovery, optional SSO, and session revocation.
- Organization, user, employee, department, role, and permission data model.
- Relational persistence with transactional approval and payroll state changes.
- Object storage with malware scanning and access-controlled document URLs.
- Notifications by in-app, email, and configurable provider integrations.
- Immutable audit events for access, approvals, profile changes, documents, payroll, and settings.
- Backups, retention and deletion controls, privacy notices, consent records, and breach response.
- Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) review for employee records and personal data.
- Automated tests for role isolation, approval transitions, payroll visibility, file access, and audit coverage.
